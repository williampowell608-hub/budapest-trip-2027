# Harrogate to Budapest by train, April 2027

Four rail routes that get you to Budapest on Fri 30 Apr 2027, with a direct flight home to Leeds Bradford (LBA) on Mon 3 May. All totals are estimates for one adult.

## Comparison

| Option | Leave | Route | Rail | Beds | Flight | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B Cheapest | Sat 24 Apr | Brussels, Berlin (2), Prague (2), Bratislava | £133 | 9 × £40 = £360 | £87 | £580 |
| A Balanced | Sat 24 Apr | Paris (2), Munich (2), Vienna (2) | £160 | 9 × £50 = £450 | £87 | £697 |
| C Night train | Sat 24 Apr | Amsterdam (2), Nightjet, Vienna (3) | £142 | 8 × £50 = £400 | £87 | £629 |
| D Fastest | Wed 28 Apr | Brussels, Nightjet, Vienna (1) | £133 | 4 × £50 = £200 | £87 | £420 |

Rail is the sum of the lowest advance "from" fares quoted by operators or Seat61, converted at €1 = £0.85 (assumption). Option C assumes £60 for Eurostar to Amsterdam (not checked) and a €49.90 couchette. Bed costs are rough budget figures and were not researched. Flight is Ryanair's lowest May fare, no bags. Budapest gets three nights in every option. None of the April 2027 dates could be priced yet.

## Option B: Cheapest (recommended, about £580)
- **Sat 24 Apr: Harrogate to London to Brussels.** LNER from £23.30, then Eurostar to Brussels (~2h) from £51. Night in Brussels.
- **Sun 25 Apr: Brussels to Berlin.** ICE via Cologne, ~6h40, DB Sparpreis Europa from €24.99. Night in Berlin.
- **Mon 26 Apr: Berlin.** 2nd night.
- **Tue 27 Apr: Berlin to Prague.** Czech Railways via the Elbe valley, ~4h15, from €18.99. (Mondays have trackwork cancellations until Oct 2027.) Night in Prague.
- **Wed 28 Apr: Prague.** 2nd night.
- **Thu 29 Apr: Prague to Bratislava.** ČD railjet, ~4h, from €15. Night in Bratislava.
- **Fri 30 Apr: Bratislava to Budapest.** ~2h20, from €10.50 (MÁV). Three nights in Budapest.
- **Mon 3 May: Fly BUD to LBA.** Ryanair direct, from £86.99.

## Option A: Balanced (about £697)
- **Sat 24 Apr:** Harrogate to London (LNER from £23.30), Eurostar to Paris (~2h20, from £51). Night in Paris.
- **Sun 25 Apr:** Paris, 2nd night.
- **Mon 26 Apr:** Paris to Munich, direct, ~5h40, from €49.99. Night in Munich.
- **Tue 27 Apr:** Munich, 2nd night.
- **Wed 28 Apr:** Munich to Vienna, ÖBB railjet ~4h, Sparschiene from €29.99.
- **Thu 29 Apr:** Vienna, 2nd night.
- **Fri 30 Apr:** Vienna to Budapest, railjet ~2h40, from €19.90 (ÖBB) or €13 (MÁV); RegioJet from €9. Three nights in Budapest.
- **Mon 3 May:** Fly BUD to LBA, Ryanair from £86.99.

## Option C: Night train (about £629)
- **Sat 24 Apr:** Harrogate to London to Amsterdam (LNER from £23.30, Eurostar ~4h). Night in Amsterdam.
- **Sun 25 Apr:** Amsterdam, 2nd night.
- **Mon 26 Apr:** Nightjet Amsterdam to Vienna. Seat61 lists (Brussels route) from €29.90 seat, €49.90 couchette, €89.90+ sleeper; the Amsterdam train is often cheaper. Overnight on the train.
- **Tue 27 to Thu 29 Apr:** Vienna, three nights (optional day trip to Bratislava).
- **Fri 30 Apr:** Vienna to Budapest, railjet from €19.90 or RegioJet from €9. Three nights.
- **Mon 3 May:** Fly BUD to LBA, Ryanair from £86.99.

## Option D: Fastest (about £420)
- **Wed 28 Apr:** Harrogate to London to Brussels. Morning LNER from £23.30, Eurostar 13:01 from St Pancras arriving 16:06, from £51 (Seat61 advises the 13:01 as the sleeper is sometimes retimed).
- **Wed 28 Apr:** Nightjet Brussels to Vienna, departs 18:56 (Mon, Wed, Fri), arrives Vienna Hbf 09:47. Couchette from €49.90.
- **Thu 29 Apr:** Vienna.
- **Fri 30 Apr:** Vienna to Budapest, railjet ~2h40, from €19.90. Three nights in Budapest.
- **Mon 3 May:** Fly BUD to LBA, Ryanair from £86.99.

## Flight home: Budapest to Leeds Bradford, Mon 3 May 2027
Ryanair starts a daily LBA to BUD route on 29 Mar 2027, from £86.99 one-way for May (~2h40). Jet2 also flies it direct twice a week, but I couldn't confirm a Monday flight. The £86.99 is the LBA to BUD direction, so the BUD to LBA fare on 3 May still needs checking on ryanair.com. Bags cost extra. Backup: Manchester (frequent Budapest flights, direct train to Leeds).

## Interrail or single tickets?
Single tickets are cheaper. A 2nd-class Global Pass (7 days in 1 month) is €381 (~£324) and Nightjet berths and some high-speed trains need paid reservations on top. Booked early, the European legs of option B cost about £59 and option A about £86.

## Recommendation
Book option B: most cities for the least money (about £580), every leg a daytime train under 7 hours, and a scenic Berlin to Prague ride. Option A costs about £115 more for the classic sights. Book Ryanair and Eurostar now; continental trains around Nov 2026; LNER Advance around late Jan 2027.
